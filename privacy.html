Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address
  • Dream Content (dream descriptions and notes voluntarily submitted by You for analysis)

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. Usage Data may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address, device type, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data.

Use of Your Personal Data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service, including to monitor the usage of our Service.

  • To manage Your Account: to manage Your registration as a user of the Service.

  • For the performance of a contract: the development, compliance and undertaking of the purchase contract for the products or services You have purchased.

  • To contact You: To contact You by email or other forms of electronic communication regarding account verification, password resets, updates, or security notices.

  • To manage Your requests: To attend and manage Your requests to Us.

Retention of Your Personal Data

  • User Accounts: retained for the duration of your account relationship plus up to 24 months after account closure.

  • Support correspondence: up to 24 months from ticket closure.

  • Server logs: up to 24 months for security monitoring.

  • Transaction records: up to 10 years to comply with tax and financial regulations.

Delete Your Personal Data

You have the right to delete or request that We assist in deleting the Personal Data that We have collected about You. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Settings screen within the app.

Third-Party Service Providers

Payments

We will not store or collect Your payment card details. Payment information is provided directly to Our third-party payment processors.

  • Apple Store In-App Payments — Privacy Policy

  • Google Play In-App Payments — Privacy Policy

Artificial Intelligence — Dream Analysis

When You submit a dream description for analysis, that content is transmitted to Anthropic's Claude API for processing. Anthropic does not use it to train its models, and we never sell or share it with third parties. Separately, we may use your dreams and their analyses to train and improve our own dream-analysis model — but only if you explicitly opt in under Settings → Data & Privacy (off by default). You can withdraw your consent at any time, and deleting your account removes this data.

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — Privacy Policy

Text-to-Speech

If You use the audio playback feature, the text of Your dream analysis is transmitted to OpenAI's text-to-speech API to generate the audio file.

  • OpenAI — Privacy Policy

Email Delivery

Transactional emails — such as account verification and password reset links — are sent through Brevo on Our behalf. Your email address is processed solely to deliver these messages.

  • Brevo — Privacy Policy

Database and Authentication

Your account information and dream records are stored on Supabase's cloud infrastructure.

  • Supabase — Privacy Policy

In-App Purchase Management

  • RevenueCat — Privacy Policy
